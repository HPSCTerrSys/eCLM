Organic fertiliser carbon and prognostic crop rotation in eCLM

Two coupled developments on the CLM5 crop model: manure carbon and nitrogen routed through the litter and mineral pools rather than entering as nitrogen alone, and a calendar-driven rotation that switches crop type between seasons and admits winter cover crops. This page maps what the code does and which namelist options control it.

the map

Every arrow here is a mass transfer in the code. The dashed green arrow is the coupling people usually miss: manure is released on a planting event, so a crop that never plants receives no manure — even when every manure namelist value is correct.

Delivered ≠ nominal. The rate reaching the soil is three quarters of manunitro × 1000 × manure_CN_ratio. Verified against Dikopshof: nominal 144 gC m⁻² yr⁻¹ produced a measured Corg_FERT of 4.032258 × 10⁻⁵ gC m⁻² s⁻¹ over a 31-day month — exactly 108 gC m⁻² yr⁻¹, the Herbst target.

The site targets are 108 (Dikopshof), 300 (Hoosfield) and 100 (Selhausen) gC m⁻² yr⁻¹. Holding carbon at those values fixes the nitrogen that comes with it, and the two cannot both be matched at a fixed C:N — at Selhausen the implied 53 kg N ha⁻¹ is well under the ~125 kg N ha⁻¹ a 25 t ha⁻¹ application would carry. The honest lever there is manure_CN_ratio, not manunitro.

The rotation year

Three calendar decisions govern which crop stands on the patch. The switch is by date, not by phenology, which is why a late-harvested summer crop can push its successor into the following year.

31 Mar

Terminate a standing cover crop

If the crop on the patch is not this year's crop in the rotation file, it is a cover crop. It is forced through harvest by zeroing gddmaturity and huigrain — the same idiom as the cold-kill path in CNPhenologyMod. itype is deliberately left alone so that residue is partitioned with the cover crop's lf_flab / fcel / flig.

1 Apr

Switch to this year's crop

itype becomes pct_cft_cur. CropPhenology then plants it inside its own min/max planting window. A crop whose window has already closed waits until the next one opens — which is how a year can end with no harvest at all.

1 Sep

Look ahead to next year

If the patch is bare, next year's crop (pct_cft_next) is inspected and adopted only when it is autumn-sown — min_NH_planting_date > 0700. A spring-sown successor is left for the following April. A summer crop still standing is left to finish.
